This week we are taking a small breather from delta vr and making a game. The lead developer you could say is Monizjoao as this is his game idea.

Primary tasks at the start were getting all the necessary software and files installed such as - Libresprite, Godot and the Github repositorry.

![[libresprite_app.png|113]]![[godot_app.png|143]]![[github_app.png|109]]

I was given a few task to do in regards to art such as making a muzzle flash for the guns. I've yet to implement it as it was bugging out but ill try again at a later time.

![[muzzleflash_sprite.png]]

I also was put in charge of making the menu screens, like the title screen, menu screen, inventory screen, death screen and end credits.

![[titlescreen_sprite.png]]

This is the title screen, im very proud of how it came out. I had a very fun time drawing it, and adding little details to really make it pop*, it is based on a picture of a pda from what im assuming might only be the game stalker, sent by Monizjoao.

![[titlescreen_inspiration.png]]

The menu screen and death screens were also based on the same pda in a design / aesthetic choice and also to keep everything more or less in the same vibe.

![[menuscreen_sprite.png]]
![[deathscreen_sprite.png]]

In the future id like to add custom buttons to that everything is in a pixelart style, but for now to have a working demo it works well enough, and i dont think it looks all that bad, I may even say i quite like it.

![[inventory_scprite.png]]

Starting with the health bar, my teammates had lazilly implemented it in a way that it even shows up when ou are in the title screen or menu, so i changed it to only show when you are in the game and playing.

Secondly i made the inventory a 3 slot quick access bar, as for now we dont have any more consumables to use. The top is a medkit, has 2 uses, restores 5 health and does not restore beyond 10 health. The blue liquid in slot 2 is a stim, it has 2 uses, and for 15 second doubles your speed both when walking and sprinting. The last slot is a bandage, it does not yet work, Im waiting for Monizjoao to make a bleeding effect so that i could then make the logic behind when bleeding occurs, how to stop it, and how it affects you. Of course I could start doing it now but it would be easier to do it simultaneously to get a propper result.

Lastly we have the end credits, not anything amazing but enough for a demo.

![[creditscreen_sprite.png]]

I also got to create a few fun voice lines for the outlaw enemies in game, Monizjoao once again sent me references to go off, so I started, but before I could record my voice I had to install a software that would do that, so I found Audacity.

![[audacity_app.png|203]]

In my usecase it wasnt anything exclusive as anything that allows mic input would work for this. So I listened to the references that I was sent, practiced a bit, made multiple variations, tuned them a bit, bit crunched a bit, and named them with absolutely insane names.

![[voicelines.png]]

Yet somehow this ended up being the right way to name them as it almost perfectly resembles what is hear in the audios.

Next I worked to add audios to the game, it wasn't all that bad, after a small bit of rummaging through my teams unorganized mess of scripts, I found out that the outlaws have their own script, and so I put the voice line functionality in there as well.
Now when they spot you they yell one of many crazy things, and when eliminated they yell another bunch of crazy things.

![[outlaw_sprite.png|133]]
