MiniStalk is a little side project the team made for the 7th week of thirdspace.
As the name suggests its a fan game of the S.T.AL.K.E.R. series and it's more of a top down bullet hell demonstration than a proper game. It has controls, a combat system and an inventory system.
![[monizjoao/titlescreen.png]]
*Titlescreen made by Grand*

Everyone worked on the art for the game. Me specifically I did the art for the enemies, main character, guns and map sprites. I also made the "Obelisk" voice lines (which are basically the monolith).


I also designed the map which was basically just a linear little path into a boss area. Nothing crazy as this was just going to be a very short demo for the game.

![[Map from the game.png]]
*Map for the game*

There were only two guns in the game. The Makarov (the one the outlaws and the main character use) and the AKM which is the one the obelisks use.

![[PM Makarov.png]]![[AKM.png]]
*Makarov and AKM*

I was also in charge of the audio in the game which I completely butchered on the pistol audio and probably gave myself ear damage or something but I fixed it by reducing the db level

For voice acting I made the Obelisks have muffled bit crushed voices to simulate the gas masks. 
![[Bit crushing the voicelines.png]]
*Bit crushing the audio*


I also made the blood particle effect when the player or the enemies get hit which in my opinion for my first ever game it looks pretty good (Grand did the art for the blood I only applied it).

*Blood splat effect being made*
